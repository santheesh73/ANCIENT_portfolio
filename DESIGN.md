# Visual direction

The opening takes place on a weathered causeway at the edge of a fictional empire. An uninterrupted camera path carries the visitor through the frontier arch, the armillary chamber, the exhibition vault, and the final gate. Scroll position determines the camera position and direction in one continuous 3D world.

Narrative and portfolio copy sit low in the viewport. The scenery, moving pennants, and instruments fill the screen. The bottom navigation tracks landmarks, and project case studies and the complete work index have direct links. Reduced-motion and WebGL fallback modes keep the same readable chapters and navigation with static stills.

The Creator Hall uses a sunlit scriptorium and engraved survey charts. The Hall of Precision follows six sequential instruments. Shelves and a central skylight frame the Library of Knowledge, and the raised terrace opens onto the horizon. These rooms extend the existing camera path and use the same sandstone and bronze materials.
